ENGINEERING OVERVIEW

How Ground Weather Stations Operate

Automatic Weather Stations (AWS) provide real-time meteorological observations without human intervention. They continuously convert physical atmospheric phenomena into digital telemetry packets transmitted via satellite or low-power telemetry channels.

  • Radiation Shields: Multi-plate louvred Stevenson screens prevent direct sunlight from biasing temperature probes.
  • Calibrated Mast Heights: Ambient temperature is logged at standard 1.5–2 meters; wind velocity is logged at 10 meters above ground.
  • Instant Data Logging: Microcontrollers sample values every 60 seconds and compute rolling averages for early warnings.
