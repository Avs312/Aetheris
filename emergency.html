IMMEDIATE FIRST RESPONDER ACTION

First-Aid for Heatstroke

Four urgent actions while waiting for medical responders to arrive:

Step 1: Relocate to Shade

Move the affected person immediately out of direct sun into a shaded or air-conditioned space.

Step 2: Rapid Surface Cooling

Sponge cold water over skin; apply ice packs wrapped in cloth directly to neck, armpits, and groin.

Step 3: Elevate Lower Limbs

Lay the patient flat and elevate feet slightly to encourage blood circulation toward vital organs.

Step 4: Careful Hydration

Only administer sips of cool water if patient is conscious and lucid. Never force fluids if disoriented.
