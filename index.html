METEOROLOGY BASICS

What is a Meteorological Heatwave?

A heatwave is an extended period of abnormally high temperatures, typically accompanied by dry north-westerly winds known regionally as the Loo.

According to the India Meteorological Department (IMD), a heatwave in the plains is officially declared when the maximum temperature reaches at least 40°C and exceeds the historical normal by 4.5°C to 6.4°C.

Key Driving Factors:

  • Anticyclonic High Pressure: Descending dry air traps surface heat.
  • Urban Heat Island (UHI): Dense concrete absorbs and retains radiation overnight.
  • Low Soil Moisture: Limits evaporative cooling in arid agricultural belts.
