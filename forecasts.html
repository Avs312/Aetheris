METEOROLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION

Heatwave Alert Levels (IMD Protocol)

Recognized criteria for tiered public heat danger warnings.

GREEN • NORMAL

No Warning

< 40.0°C

Standard seasonal temperatures. No thermal distress expected for general populace.

YELLOW • WATCH

Be Updated

40.0°C – 42.5°C

Moderate heat. Tolerable for general public, but health caution needed for outdoor labor.

ORANGE • ALERT

Be Prepared

42.6°C – 44.5°C

Severe heat stress. High probability of heat illness and heatstroke in children & elderly.

RED • WARNING

Take Action

> 44.5°C or >2 Days

Extreme medical emergency. All outdoor activities should be suspended during noon hours.
