TARGETED AUDIENCE ADVISORIES

Tailored Protection Guidelines

Customized precautions based on daily exposure levels and community roles.

🏠

Citizens & Households

Protection for families, young children, and senior citizens.

  • Remain indoors between 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM.
  • Wear loose, light-colored, breathable cotton attire.
  • Cover windows with curtains or reflective bamboo blinds during peak sunlight.
  • Never leave children or pets inside parked automobiles.
🌾

Farmers & Outdoor Workers

Actionable adjustments for agricultural labor and field workers.

  • Shift outdoor field hours to 05:30–10:30 AM and after 4:30 PM.
  • Carry traditional ear-covering turbans, cloth caps, or umbrellas.
  • Take 15-minute shaded rest breaks every hour with ORS solution.
  • Provide shaded sheds and clean drinking water troughs for livestock.
🏛️

Local Municipal Authorities

Civic measures for city administration and health workers.

  • Activate public cooling centers in community halls and libraries.
  • Ensure operational roadside water kiosks (pyaau) in bustling markets.
  • Equip primary health centers (PHCs) with dedicated ice packs and IV fluids.
  • Coordinate with power utilities to prevent afternoon power load shedding.
CHRONOLOGICAL PRECAUTIONS

Timeline: Before, During & After a Heatwave

A phased strategy to minimize physiological stress.

PHASE 01 • BEFORE

Preparation

Stock Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS), glucose, and clean drinking water. Check fan and cooler operations. Verify that elderly neighbors have assistance.

PHASE 02 • DURING

Peak Heat Defense

Drink water every 20 minutes even if not thirsty. Use damp washcloths on the neck and forehead. Keep physical exertion minimal in unconditioned rooms.

PHASE 03 • AFTER

Recovery & Reset

Open cross-ventilation windows once evening breezes cool down. Continue electrolyte intake. Watch for lingering fatigue, dizziness, or dehydration signs.

HYDRATION ESSENTIALS

Preventing Heat Exhaustion

Dehydration reduces the body's natural sweating mechanism, quickly spiking internal core temperature.

Recommended Cooling Fluids:

ORS Solution Coconut Water Chaas (Buttermilk) Aam Panna Lemon Shikanji

Avoid: High-sugar carbonated sodas, concentrated caffeine, and alcohol, as they act as diuretics and accelerate fluid loss.

MEDICAL ADVISORIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Click to expand guidance on clinical heat mitigation.

Heat Exhaustion vs. Heatstroke
Heat Exhaustion: Heavy sweating, pale clammy skin, weakness, dizziness, and nausea. Managed with rest and cool fluids.

Heatstroke (Medical Emergency): High body temperature (>40°C), hot red dry skin (no sweating), rapid pulse, confusion, or fainting. Requires immediate emergency medical care (Call 108).
How to Keep Rooms Cool Without Air Conditioning
Hang damp white sheets or vetiver (khus) mats over sunny windows. Use ceiling fans together with cross-ventilation during late nights. Place bowls of ice water in front of table fans for natural evaporative air currents.
Protecting Vulnerable Family Members
Infants and seniors over 65 years have slower thermoregulation responses. Monitor room temperatures closely, ensure hourly hydration checks, and sponge their limbs with lukewarm water if room fans are insufficient.
