ANALYSIS GUIDE

Why Forecasts Differ from Ground Reality

Computer models calculate atmospheric conditions in wide 5-kilometer grid boxes. Ground-level thermometers, however, measure real street-level air influenced by microclimates.

  • Asphalt Absorption: Heavy roads retain solar energy, raising ground temperatures slightly above open-field models.
  • Localized Gusts: Sudden afternoon wind changes can disperse heat faster than standard grids predict.
  • Vegetation Canopy: Tree-shaded avenues can record 1°C lower than surrounding concrete zones.
